KayKit Medieval Builder CC0 Free kaykit.itch.io · staged at assets/staging/kaykit-medieval-builder/

Castle courtyard with watchtowers, walls, gate — surrounded by houses, barracks, market, farm, lumbermill, and tree/rock dressing.

Structures used in this scene castle.gltf.glb watchtower.gltf.glb wall_straight.gltf.glb wall_corner.gltf.glb wall_gate.gltf.glb house.gltf.glb barracks.gltf.glb market.gltf.glb farm_plot.gltf.glb lumbermill.gltf.glb mill.gltf.glb well.gltf.glb Also in the pack (not shown) archeryrange.gltf.glb bridge.gltf.glb bridge_roofed.gltf.glb mine.gltf.glb mountain.gltf.glb watermill.gltf.glb forest.gltf.glb detail_forestA/B.gltf.glb detail_treeA/B/C.gltf.glb detail_hill.gltf.glb detail_rocks.gltf.glb wall_hexCornerA/B.gltf.glb wall_gate_closed.gltf.glb + 84 square tile variants

Scene notes

  • Scale: all objects at 0.55 (approved-kit default). Castle + watchtowers feel right together.
  • Ground: procedural (SceneBuilder.ground) with a stone-coloured courtyard platform. No tile models used.
  • Lighting: default SceneBuilder (warm tungsten + cool fill). Shadows cast correctly.
  • Tiles: 84 square tile variants exist (forest, rock, road, water edge) but weren't used — they showed up too large at scale 1.0 in initial tests.
Review questions
  1. Does the castle + wall + watchtower combination read as a proper fortified settlement?
  2. Are the building scales believable next to each other (house vs barracks vs castle)?
  3. Should the courtyard use stone tile models instead of a flat platform mesh?
  4. Any buildings missing that would complete this set?

Four tree types clustered in quadrants — round broadleaf (type 1), layered cone (type 2), multi-sphere (type 3), triangular pine (type 4). Bare/autumn trees at edges. Rock outcroppings center-right. Bush clusters in the clearing.

Review questions

KayKit Adventurers 2.0 CC0 Free kaykit.itch.io · staged at assets/staging/kaykit-adventurers/

All 6 character types with team-colour tints and matching weapons. Left to right: Knight (blue), Barbarian (red), Ranger (gold + bow), Rogue Hooded (green + dagger), Mage (purple + staff), Rogue (grey + sword).

Characters (6) Knight.glb Barbarian.glb Ranger.glb Rogue_Hooded.glb Mage.glb Rogue.glb Weapons + items (31 models, selection shown) sword_1handed.gltf sword_2handed.gltf axe_1handed.gltf axe_2handed.gltf bow_withString.gltf crossbow_2handed.gltf staff.gltf dagger.gltf wand.gltf shield_round.gltf shield_square.gltf shield_spikes.gltf spellbook_open.gltf quiver.gltf smokebomb.gltf

Scene notes

  • Scale: characters at 0.36 (approved-kit default). This makes them feel like figurines on a board, not life-size characters.
  • Tinting: SceneBuilder.tint_node() applies team colour recursively across all meshes. Works well.
  • Weapons: placed as separate floating props next to each character — not rigged/attached. Rigging is in the Animations/ subfolder (FBX) but not wired up yet.
  • Animations: FBX rig files exist (Rig_Medium_General.fbx, Rig_Medium_MovementBasic.fbx) — not used in any experiment yet.
Review questions
  1. Does the character scale (0.36) feel right for board-game figurines?
  2. Which characters look most useful for the game concepts in the pipeline?
  3. Should any characters be excluded from the approved kit? (E.g., Rogue vs Rogue Hooded overlap.)
  4. Worth wiring up the walk animation, even just as a looping idle?

Famous Game Recreations

All three KayKit packs combined · 5 scenes · genre variety test

Same asset packs, assembled to recreate the silhouette of famous games. Useful for understanding layout conventions and seeing how far the assets stretch across genres.

Clash of Clans — Base Defense

castle.gltf.glb watchtower.gltf.glb ×8 wall_straight.gltf.glb ×24 wall_gate.gltf.glb ×2 barracks.gltf.glb archeryrange.gltf.glb well.gltf.glb Knight.glb (blue defenders) Barbarian.glb ×10 (red horde)
  • Two concentric rings read clearly as a CoC base layout. The wall_straight + watchtower combo does most of the heavy lifting.
  • Attackers are slightly clipped at bottom — a slightly larger cam_size (14) would frame them better.
  • Inner ring buildings (barracks, archery range) disappear behind the castle. They're there but invisible. Worth raising buildings or using an outer ring.

Helm's Deep — Siege

wall_straight.gltf.glb ×18 wall_gate_closed.gltf.glb watchtower.gltf.glb ×8 castle.gltf.glb (Hornburg) barracks.gltf.glb ×2 Knight.glb ×6 (defenders) Barbarian.glb ×20 (horde) Tree_Bare_1 (cliff atmosphere) detail_hill.gltf.glb
  • Best scene. The wall spanning edge-to-edge with 20 attackers advancing in waves reads immediately as an epic siege. The bare trees add real atmosphere.
  • wall_gate_closed (vs wall_gate) is the right call here — you want it shut.
  • The barracks behind the wall are mostly hidden — they serve as implied depth, which works.

Stardew Valley — Cozy Farm

farm_plot.gltf.glb ×2 watermill.gltf.glb market.gltf.glb mill.gltf.glb well.gltf.glb house.gltf.glb ×2 Tree_1..4 (forest pack) ×13 Bush_1..4 ×5 Ranger.glb (golden farmer)
  • The green ground override is essential — completely changes the mood vs brown dirt.
  • Trees framing the corners create the "cozy enclosure" feeling that Stardew lives on.
  • The buildings feel a bit sparse in the center. Real Stardew has much denser farm plots — adding 3-4 more farm_plot tiles would help.
  • The golden farmer is barely visible. Scale 0.38 is too small at cam_size=10; try 0.44.

Fire Emblem — Bridge Battle

bridge.gltf.glb bridge_roofed.gltf.glb watchtower.gltf.glb ×2 Knight.glb ×3 (blue front) Ranger.glb ×3 (gold back row) Rogue_Hooded.glb (blue flanker) Barbarian.glb ×5 (red) Mage.glb (purple commander) Rogue.glb (red flanker) Tree_1..4 ×12 (forest flanks) Rock_1, Rock_2
  • Two armies in clear formation facing each other reads perfectly as a tactics RPG. The Mage commander in purple stands out well from the back row.
  • The rogues hiding in the trees on each flank are a nice detail — you can spot them if you look.
  • The bridge model renders as a very flat pale arc — fine for layout purposes but wouldn't pass as a real bridge without some height under it.
  • Strongest proof that these assets can support a tactics game like Bad North / Into the Breach.

Age of Empires II — Full Civilization

castle.gltf.glb wall_straight.gltf.glb ×4 watchtower.gltf.glb ×2 wall_gate.gltf.glb barracks.gltf.glb archeryrange.gltf.glb farm_plot.gltf.glb ×2 market.gltf.glb mill.gltf.glb lumbermill.gltf.glb watermill.gltf.glb mine.gltf.glb mountain.gltf.glb house.gltf.glb ×2 Knight.glb ×2, Ranger.glb detail_forestA/B, forest.gltf.glb
  • This scene uses every structure type in the medieval builder pack — useful as a catalogue shot showing the full scope.
  • The distinct districts (military right, farming left, production back, castle center) mirror real AoE base layouts.
  • The mountain + mine in the corner reads immediately as a resource node.
  • The detail_forestA asset renders as a teal-blue clump — interesting palette but inconsistent with the warm ground. Worth investigating whether it has a material override.
